## Limits

The sensitivity envelopes are chosen assumptions, not empirical confidence intervals. Tests confirm implementation and arithmetic, not predictive validity or external-source accuracy. The responsive harness uses constrained browser frames, not physical mobile hardware. Source IDs are checked against the dataset; perpetual availability of external websites is not guaranteed.
